About AxisMED

The Innovation Engine Born at Carle Illinois

AxisMED is a student-founded, independently operated biomedical innovation incubator. We exist at the intersection of medicine, engineering, and business — bringing together multidisciplinary teams to attack real, validated clinical unmet needs.

We are not a class or a club. AxisMED is a pre-incubation program that gives student entrepreneurs the infrastructure, mentorship, and competitive pathways to build ventures that matter — before they graduate.

Structured 5-Stage Design Review Curriculum
Multidisciplinary Teams: Medicine + Engineering + Business
NSF I-Corps Lean Launchpad Integration
IP Guidance via UIUC Office of Technology Management
Pipeline to Cozad, iVenture, and EnterpriseWorks
NDA-Protected Innovation from Day One
The Program

Five Design Reviews.
One Investable Company.

A structured academic-year journey from clinical problem statement to investor-ready pitch — guided by faculty advisors, industry mentors, and an experienced exec board.

DR1
October
Problem Statement
  • Identify & validate clinical need
  • Customer discovery begins
  • Problem statement deliverable
  • 7-min presentation to exec
DR2
November
Market & Customer Discovery
  • I-Corps Lean Launchpad
  • 10+ stakeholder interviews
  • Value proposition articulated
  • TAM / SAM / SOM estimation
DR3
December
IP & Commercialization
  • IP landscape review
  • Competitive analysis
  • Business model canvas
  • Regulatory pathway ID
DR4
January
Prototype & Solution
  • Initial prototype / PoC
  • Technical feasibility confirmed
  • Defined value proposition
  • Advisor feedback incorporated
DR5
March
Final Pitch
  • Investor-ready pitch deck
  • Cozad Competition entry
  • Demo Day presentation
  • Next steps & funding strategy

Get Involved

Ready to Build Something That Matters?

AxisMED accepts applications each fall. Teams are formed from students across medicine, engineering, and business who are serious about solving real clinical problems.

1

Apply to a Role

Submit your application as a Team Leader or Team Member. Applications open each August.

2

Orientation & Team Formation

Attend orientation, pitch your clinical problem, and form your multidisciplinary team.

3

Begin Design Reviews

Work through the five-stage curriculum with exec support, faculty advisors, and industry mentors.

4

Pitch on Demo Day

Present your investor-ready company at Demo Day and enter Cozad New Venture Challenge.

Team Leader

Lead a team through the full AxisMED curriculum. Bring a clinical problem or work with one assigned by the exec board. Requires commitment and ownership.

Team Member

Join an existing team as an engineering, business, or pre-med contributor. No idea required — just drive and curiosity.

Become an Advisor

Industry experts, physicians, investors, and operators: bring your experience into the room. Advisors mentor teams through Design Reviews and provide real-world validation.
