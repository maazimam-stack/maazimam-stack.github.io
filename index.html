Our Mission

Filling the Gap Between
Academia and Innovation

AxisMED's pre-incubation program connects students with the tools necessary to build early-stage healthcare ventures — bridging the University's academic resources, Carle Health, and business incubators.

Born from the mission of Carle Illinois College of Medicine — the world's first engineering-based medical school — AxisMED brings together future physicians, engineers, and business leaders to tackle the most pressing unmet needs in healthcare. We follow the Stanford Biodesign methodology, combining clinical immersion, entrepreneurial education, and hands-on mentorship.

The AxisMED Curriculum

From Problem to Pitch in One Academic Year

Our five-stage Design Review process takes teams from raw clinical observation to an investor-ready pitch deck — with workshops, mentor meetings, and peer review at every step.

DR1

Problem Statement

Define the clinical need. Who is affected, where, and how severely?

DR2

Market & Customer Discovery

Validate the need through stakeholder interviews and I-Corps training.

DR3

IP & Commercialization

Explore the IP landscape, competitive analysis, and business model.

DR4

Prototype & Solution

Build and present your initial prototype with a defined value proposition.

DR5

Final Pitch

Investor-ready deck, demo day at Cozad, and pathway to continued funding.

📅

Weekly Workshops

Structured workshops covering needs assessment, value propositions, IP/regulatory, market analysis, and pitching — led by faculty and industry mentors.

🤝

Advisor Matching

Every team is paired with a clinical advisor, engineering mentor, and executive board liaison for ongoing 1-on-1 guidance throughout the year.

🏆

Cozad & Beyond

Top teams compete in the Cozad New Venture Challenge and connect with the broader UIUC entrepreneurship ecosystem — including iVenture and EnterpriseWorks.

Ecosystem

Our Partners & Collaborators

AxisMED is embedded in the UIUC innovation ecosystem — connecting teams with resources, mentors, and pathways to funding.

🏛️ Carle Illinois College of Medicine

The world's first engineering-based medical school and AxisMED's academic home. CIMED faculty serve as advisors and champions of our integration into the medical curriculum.

🏢 Gies College of Business

Business students and faculty from Gies join AxisMED teams and workshops to provide financial modeling, market analysis, and commercialization expertise.

⚙️ Grainger College of Engineering

Engineering students across BME, ECE, and CS disciplines provide the technical backbone for AxisMED's device and software teams.

🧪 Illinois I-Corps

AxisMED team leaders are integrated into Illinois I-Corps' Lean Launchpad program for customer discovery training and NSF I-Corps eligibility.

🚀 Cozad New Venture Challenge

AxisMED teams are guided toward the Cozad competition — UIUC's flagship startup pitch event — as the capstone of their year-long program.

⚖️ UIUC IP Clinic

The Office of Technology Management and IP Clinic provide patent, trademark, and licensing guidance to protect every team's intellectual property from day one.
